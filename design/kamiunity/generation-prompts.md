## Logo

Use case: logo-brand.
Design one original, polished logo for "kamiunity", a private, completely free university application planner for students planning international graduate study. This is a new identity, not an existing institution. Exact wordmark spelling: kamiunity (k a m i u n i t y), all lowercase.
Asset: standalone horizontal symbol + wordmark lockup, centered with generous clear space, target canvas 1536 x 1024. It should work on a web app, PWA icon, and student application documents.
Create a distinctive compact K monogram: two simple interlocking, softly rounded forms with precise negative space suggesting an open doorway and two paths meeting, conveying individual progress and helpful connection. It must read as an intentional K at small size. Pair it with custom-feeling, confident humanist sans serif lowercase lettering; balanced kerning, gently rounded terminals, thoughtful geometry. Logo mark to the left of the wordmark. One coherent logo only.
Palette grounded in the existing app: deep teal #176d6e and dark ink #243133, warm stone #f1ede8 when viewed on a surface. Primary lockup uses teal symbol and dark ink wordmark. Flat, crisp, vector-like graphic edges with no lighting, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D. True transparent background with alpha; no simulated checkerboard. No extra words, no slogans, no watermark, no mockup stationery, no badge, no graduation cap, no generic compass, no arrows, no literal globe. This should feel like a distinctive contemporary academic planning brand, friendly and serious.
